# Hi, I'm Saif Khan 👋

[![Typing SVG](https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&pause=500&width=550&height=60&lines=AI+Engineer;Computer+Vision+%2B+VLM+Pipelines;Exploring+the+AI+Frontier;Machines+See%2C+Models+Think%2C+I+Create)](https://git.io/typing-svg)

I'm an **AI Engineer** building production-ready AI systems, specializing in **computer vision**, **VLMs**, and **MLOps**. I love turning research ideas into practical products that solve real-world problems.

I like the messy part of AI, where a model works in a notebook and then has to survive real data.

---

## 🔭 What I keep coming back to

- 🧬 Building vision models that are faster and more accurate for a specific problem, including custom YOLO architectures instead of off-the-shelf ones
- 🔬 Making vision and VLMs work on medical images, where mistakes matter
- 🚁 Teaching drones to see and react, from hand-gesture control to follow-me tracking in simulation (ROS 2 + PX4)

---

## 🌟 Open Source Contributions

I enjoy fixing things in tools I use. I've had PRs merged into:

- **[ultralytics/ultralytics](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics)** 
- **[roboflow/supervision](https://github.com/roboflow/supervision)** 
- **[PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR)** 
- **[obss/SAHI](https://github.com/obss/sahi)**

I also maintain **[pixpick](https://github.com/K-saif/pixpick)**, an interactive vision-input toolkit for CV frameworks (YOLO, SAM2, Supervision), published on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pixpick/) with ~30K downloads.
